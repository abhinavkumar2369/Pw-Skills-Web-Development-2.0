The benefits of using semantic tags in our webpages are –  


1. Maintenance and Updating:

When you need to modify or redesign your website, semantic tags provide clear guidance on where certain types of content are located,
making updates more efficient.


2. SEO:

Search engines can better understand the content and hierarchy of your webpage when you use semantic tags.
This can positively impact search engine rankings and make your content more discoverable.


3. Accessibility:

Semantic tags provide a clear structure that aids screen readers and other assistive technologies in understanding the content.
This ensures a better experience for users with disabilities.
