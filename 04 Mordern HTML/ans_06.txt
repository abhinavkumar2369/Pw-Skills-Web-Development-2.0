5 Semantic tags in HTML are --

1. <header>:

Represents the introductory content or a container for a group of introductory elements.
It often includes a site's logo, navigation, and sometimes a heading.


2. <nav> :

Defines a section of navigation links, typically used for menus that lead to
different parts of the website or related pages.


3. <main> :

Signifies the main content of the web page.
There should be only one <main>' element per page, and it contains the primary content that is unique to that page.


4. <article> :

Represents a self-contained piece of content that can be distributed and reused independently.
It could be a blog post, news article, forum post, etc.


5. <footer> :

Defines the footer section of a webpage or a section within a page footer.
It usually contains copyright information, contact details, and links to related resources.
